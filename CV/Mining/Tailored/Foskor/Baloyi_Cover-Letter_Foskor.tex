\textbf{Application: Senior Data Engineer or Artificial Intelligence Engineer}

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for a Senior Data Engineer or Artificial Intelligence Engineer role at
Foskor. I bring more than five years of experience in data engineering, production AI and
statistical modelling, an MSc in Mathematical Statistics completed with distinction, and a PhD in
Mathematical Statistics starting at the University of Pretoria. My strongest recent work is in
heavy-industry operations, and I have worked on solutions for companies that manufacture explosives
for the mining industry, such as AECI. Foskor's chain from phosphate rock mining in Phalaborwa, by rail to phosphoric acid and fertiliser production in Richards Bay, is exactly the kind of multi-site planning problem my optimisation and data work is built for.

At 4S4 I lead development of an enterprise demand and production-planning platform for a
heavy-industry manufacturer. The platform extracts data from SAP S/4HANA into a warehouse, explodes multi-level
bills of materials against finite work-centre capacity, and uses mixed-integer programming
and Monte Carlo simulation to find bottlenecks and recommend feasible production plans. I also built a governed AI planning co-pilot on top
of it, in which every action is audited and confirmed by a person before it is applied. Before this I
built cloud data and machine learning systems on Azure, AWS and Databricks for three years.

For Foskor, this combination of data, AI and statistics can deliver solutions such as:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.2in,itemsep=1pt,topsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Operational data platforms} joining SAP, plant, fleet and sensor data in one governed lakehouse.
    \item \textbf{Planning optimisation} for production schedules, equipment, shifts, stockpiles and logistics.
    \item \textbf{Forecasting and predictive maintenance} for throughput, energy and equipment failure.
    \item \textbf{AI assistants for operations and safety} over procedures and incident records, with human sign-off.
    \item \textbf{Risk and scenario analysis} with Monte Carlo simulation of prices, disruption and capital.
\end{itemize}

AI is changing how the world works. Generative and agentic AI now read documents, write code and
support decisions, while machine learning on sensor and operational data lets industries predict
failures, optimise energy and plan with far more precision than before. Much of the work in a mine is
still manual: compiling shift and production reports, reconciling spreadsheets, checking permits and
re-entering data between systems. Automated pipelines and AI agents can do this in minutes, which speeds
up every process and frees people for the work that needs judgement. In mining, this means safer
operations, higher throughput and lower cost per tonne, but only for companies whose data is reliable
and whose models are trusted. That is where I add value: I build the data foundations, the production
AI systems and the statistical validation together. I validate models out of sample, quantify their
uncertainty and explain their outputs, so that engineers and managers can trust and act on them, and
I communicate results clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

My CV is attached. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to Foskor's data and
AI programme, and I am available to start on two weeks' notice.
