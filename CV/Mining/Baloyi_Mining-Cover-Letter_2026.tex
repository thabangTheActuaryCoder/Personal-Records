\textbf{Application: Senior Data Engineer or Artificial Intelligence Engineer}

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for a Senior Data Engineer or Artificial Intelligence Engineer role in your
organisation. I bring more than five years of experience in data engineering, production AI and
statistical modelling, an MSc in Mathematical Statistics completed with distinction, and a PhD in
Mathematical Statistics starting at the University of Pretoria. My strongest recent work is in
heavy-industry operations, which is where I would like to apply it next.

At 4S4 I lead development of an enterprise demand and production-planning platform for Rheinmetall
Denel Munition. The platform extracts data from SAP S/4HANA into a warehouse, explodes multi-level
bills of materials against finite work-centre capacity, and uses mixed-integer programming,
critical-path analysis and Monte Carlo lead-time simulation to find bottlenecks and recommend feasible
production, overtime, transfer and delivery plans. I also built a governed AI planning co-pilot on top
of it, in which every action is audited and confirmed by a person before it is applied. Before this I
spent three years building cloud data and machine learning systems on Azure, AWS and Databricks, and
two years at Discovery developing and monitoring models on large operational datasets.

For a mining operation, this combination of data, AI and statistics can deliver solutions such as:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.2in,itemsep=1pt,topsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Operational data platforms} that bring SAP, plant historian, fleet and sensor data into
          one governed lakehouse with tested pipelines and trusted reporting.
    \item \textbf{Mine and plant planning optimisation} for production schedules, equipment and shift
          allocation, stockpiles and logistics, with the trade-offs made visible to planners.
    \item \textbf{Forecasting and predictive maintenance} for throughput, energy demand and equipment
          failure, with uncertainty ranges rather than single numbers.
    \item \textbf{AI assistants for operations and safety} that answer questions over procedures,
          incident reports and maintenance records, with sources cited and human sign-off.
    \item \textbf{Risk and scenario analysis} using Monte Carlo simulation for commodity prices,
          supply disruption and capital decisions.
\end{itemize}

What sets my work apart is statistical rigour. I validate models out of sample, quantify their
uncertainty and explain their outputs, so that engineers and managers can trust and act on them.
I also communicate results clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

My CV is attached. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to your data and
AI programme, and I am available to start on two weeks' notice.
